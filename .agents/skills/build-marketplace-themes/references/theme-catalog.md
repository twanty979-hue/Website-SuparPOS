## Commercial sequencing

Build all requested entries. For a staged 100-theme rollout, begin with priority A themes across different verticals rather than completing one category at a time. This produces a commercially diverse first release and better marketplace demand signals.

Before public release, validate the proposed product name against relevant trademarks and marketplace naming conflicts. Rename the product and its canonical filename/`theme_mode` together before launch; never allow route and database identifiers to drift.
